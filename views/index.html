Whole-body cryotherapy

Powercab Performance · Storm

Three minutes at −110°C.

Why it’s trending

Cold has become the most visible recovery ritual of the decade, from elite sport to members’ clubs. The global cryotherapy market reached about $366m in 2025 and keeps growing around 5% a year, led by electric cryochambers.

Source: IMARC Group, Cryotherapy Market 2026–2034

What it does

An electric cold chamber surrounds the body with dry air at −110°C for two to three minutes. Guests report less muscle soreness and inflammation, a lift in energy and mood, and better sleep. One client per session, tailored programme per client.

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy

Oxygen under pressure, in a lounger.

Why it’s trending

Once confined to hospitals, HBOT is now the machine longevity guests ask about first, and a regular subject in mainstream wellness media. It is also one of the highest earners per square metre on a wellness floor.

Breathing concentrated oxygen at 2 to 2.5 atmospheres dissolves far more oxygen into the blood plasma, supporting recovery, tissue repair and energy. Sessions run 60 to 90 minutes, fully automated: pressure profile, oxygen delivery and timing.
